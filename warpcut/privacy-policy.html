Privacy Policy

Summary

Warpcut edits videos entirely on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your videos or any other data. There is no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and editing does not need an internet connection. Warpcut is free to download; exported videos carry a small Warpcut watermark, which you can remove with a one-time in-app purchase (Warpcut Plus). Apple handles the payment and the app only checks, through Apple, whether Plus is active.

What is stored on your device

Only what you enter or choose: projects (the list of clips, cuts, transitions, filters, music and settings you choose), copies of the videos and audio files you import, and your preferences. These are your own videos and may contain personal content. It is stored in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection while your device is locked.

Notifications

Warpcut does not send notifications.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, Warpcut asks iOS to verify you. iOS performs the check; Warpcut never receives or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode, only whether the check succeeded.

Photos and documents

Videos and audio: you choose them with the system photo picker or file picker; Warpcut only receives what you choose and keeps a copy inside the app. Exported videos are saved to your photo library only when you tap Save, using the add-only permission. Analysis for Smart Shorten (scene changes, motion, faces, sound level) runs on your device and the results stay inside the project.

Exporting and backups

Nothing leaves your device unless you decide. When you export, Warpcut creates a video file on your iPhone and, if you choose, saves it to your photo library or hands it to the destination you pick in the iOS share sheet (Messages, AirDrop, Mail or another app). Warpcut cannot control what that destination does with the file. Like any app, Warpcut's data is also part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have device backups turned on; imported video and audio copies are excluded from that backup. That backup is managed by Apple under your Apple Account.

Permissions summary

Photo library (add-only, optional, only to save exported videos) and the photo and file pickers (optional). No location, contacts, camera, microphone or tracking.

Deletion

Delete a project inside the app, or delete the app to remove all projects and imported copies. The developer holds no copy and cannot recover your data. Export and backup files you created yourself stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Warpcut does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Note

Warpcut is an editing tool. Make sure you have the rights to the videos and audio you edit and share.
