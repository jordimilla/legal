Quick answers

How do I remove the watermark? Open Warpcut Plus from Settings or the export screen and buy it once. Use Restore Purchases if you reinstall or change phone.

Where are my projects stored? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them, so export videos you want to keep.

Is the music free to use? The music included in the app is original or released under CC0. See the app for details.
