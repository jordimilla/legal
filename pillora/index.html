Quick answers

Is my information private? Yes. It is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

Why didn't I get a reminder? Check Settings > Notifications > Pillora in iOS, and Focus or Do Not Disturb. Open Pillora once: reminders are rebuilt whenever the app opens or a schedule changes.

I recorded a dose by mistake. On the Today screen tap Change > Undo (or Mark as skipped). From History you can open any day and correct it.

How do I move to a new phone? Use an iPhone backup or Quick Start. You can also export a full backup (Settings > Export, backup and restore) and restore it in Pillora on the new phone.

How do I get a report for my doctor? Settings > Export, backup and restore > Medication report (PDF).

Does Pillora give medical advice? No. It is an organizer; always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
