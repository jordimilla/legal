Privacy Policy

Summary

Pillora keeps everything you enter on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your data. There is no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not need an internet connection to work. The app is sold as a one-time purchase through the App Store; Apple handles payment.

What is stored on your device

Only what you enter or choose: medications (name, strength, form, instructions, notes, an optional photo of the box), schedules, treatment dates, remaining quantities, reminder settings, and your intake history (which doses you marked as taken, skipped or postponed, and when). This is health-related information. It is stored in a file inside the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection while your device is locked.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Pillora schedules local reminders on your iPhone. They are created and delivered by iOS; nothing is sent over the internet. Notification text can include the medication name, strength and amount, which may be visible on your Lock Screen. You can turn on "Hide medication names in notifications" in Settings. Reminders can be delayed or silenced by iOS settings (Focus, Low Power Mode, Do Not Disturb), so they are not a guarantee.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, Pillora asks iOS to verify you. iOS performs the check; Pillora never receives or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode, only whether the check succeeded.

Photos

If you add a photo of a medication box, you choose it with the system photo picker. Pillora only receives the photo you choose, keeps a compressed copy inside the app and does not access the rest of your library.

Exporting, sharing and backups

Nothing leaves your device unless you decide. In Settings you can export the medication list or intake history (CSV), a medication report (PDF) and a full backup file (JSON, including photos). These files are created on your iPhone and handed to the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet (for example Files, AirDrop, Mail or another app). Pillora cannot control what that destination does with them, and the files are not encrypted, so share them only with people and places you trust. To restore a backup you select a file with the system file picker. Like any app, Pillora's data is also part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have device backups turned on; that backup is managed by Apple under your Apple Account.

Permissions summary

Notifications (optional), Face ID (optional) and the photo picker (optional). No location, contacts, microphone, camera, health data or tracking.

Deletion

Use Delete all my data in Settings, or delete the app. The developer holds no copy and cannot recover your data. Backup and export files you created yourself stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Pillora does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Pillora is an organizer. It does not give medical advice, suggest doses, diagnose conditions or change any treatment, and it is not a regulated medical device. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.
