Quick answers

How do I give my vet a copy of the records? Open the pet, tap the menu and choose Create health record (PDF), then share it from the share sheet.

How do I move to a new phone? Use an iPhone backup or Quick Start, or export a full backup (Settings > Backup and restore) and restore it in Vetfolio on the new phone.

Why did I not get a reminder? Check that notifications are allowed for Vetfolio in iOS Settings and that the vaccination or appointment has a date.
