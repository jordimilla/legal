Quick answers

How do I add routines, people and memories? Use the tabs at the bottom: My day, People and Memories, then tap +.

Why don't I get reminders? Check Settings > Notifications > Hearthday in iOS, and that reminders are on in the app.

How do I move to a new phone? Create a backup file in Caregiver > Settings > Backup, then restore it on the new phone.
