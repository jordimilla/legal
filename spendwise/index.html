Quick answers

Is my information private? Yes. It is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no bank connection. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I change the currency? The base currency (used for totals and budgets) can be changed in Settings > Currency and exchange rates. An account's own currency is chosen when you create it and cannot be changed afterwards; create a new account and move the money with a transfer.

My balance is wrong. Open the account and tap Correct the balance. Spendwise adds a dated adjustment, so your past reports do not change.

How do I import my bank's CSV? Settings > Import and backup > Import transactions from CSV. The first row must name the columns Date and Amount (optionally Type, Account, Category, Merchant, Note). You see a preview with any problems before anything is added, and rows already in Spendwise are skipped.

How do I move to a new phone? Use an iPhone backup or Quick Start. You can also export a full backup (Settings > Import and backup) and restore it in Spendwise on the new phone.
