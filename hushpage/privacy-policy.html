Privacy Policy

Summary

Hushpage keeps everything you write on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your data. There is no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and the app does not need an internet connection to work.

What is stored on your device

Only what you enter or choose: your journal entries (titles, text, mood, dates), the names of your journals, photos you attach, voice notes you record, and your settings (such as the reminder time). Entries are stored in a file inside the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection while your device is locked.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the lock, Hushpage asks iOS to verify you. iOS performs the check; Hushpage never receives or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode, only whether the check succeeded.

What leaves the device

Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to export a PDF (text and photos; voice notes are not included) or share the text of an entry, you decide who receives it; the exported file is not encrypted. Purchases are handled by Apple; Hushpage only learns whether Hushpage Plus is unlocked. Like any app, Hushpage's data is part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; that backup is managed by Apple under your Apple Account.

Permissions

Notifications (optional, for the daily reminder you set; the text never contains anything you wrote), Face ID (optional, for the lock), the microphone (optional, only when you tap Record voice note; recordings are stored inside the app, not in your Voice Memos or Photos), and the system photo picker (the app only receives the photos you choose; it does not get access to your whole library). No location, contacts, camera or tracking. Voice notes are never transcribed or analysed and never leave the device.

Deletion

Use Delete all my data in Settings, or delete the app. The developer holds no copy and cannot recover your data.

Children

Hushpage does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
