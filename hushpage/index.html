Quick answers

Is my journal private? Yes. It is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn on Face ID? Settings > Privacy > Lock with Face ID (part of Hushpage Plus). If Face ID fails, your iPhone passcode unlocks the journal.

I bought Plus on another iPhone. Open Hushpage Plus in Settings and tap Restore purchase.

How do I record a voice note? Open an entry, tap Record a voice note (part of Hushpage Plus) and allow the microphone the first time. Notes can be up to five minutes and are stored only on your iPhone.

Why don't I get reminders? Check Settings > Notifications > Hushpage in iOS, and that the daily reminder is on in the app.

How do I move to a new phone? Use an iPhone backup or Quick Start; Hushpage's data is included. Export to PDF in Settings makes a printable copy.
