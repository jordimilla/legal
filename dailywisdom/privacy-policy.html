Privacy Policy

Daily Wisdom does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your preferences, favorites and history are stored only on your device (and in the app group shared with the widget). Reminders are local notifications scheduled by iOS on your device; the app never contacts a server.
