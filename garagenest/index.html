Quick answers

Why is my cost per km not shown? It needs at least 50 km of recorded distance. Add a mileage reading or more records.

How do I move to a new phone? Use an iPhone backup or Quick Start, or export a full backup (Settings > Data) and restore it in Garagenest on the new phone.

Why did I not get a reminder? Check that notifications are allowed for Garagenest in iOS Settings and that the reminder has a due date or mileage.
