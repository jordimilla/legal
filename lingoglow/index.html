LingoGlow

Every glance, a new word.

LingoGlow turns the moments you glance at your iPhone into small language lessons. A new word, expression, phrasal verb, or idiom appears on your Lock Screen, so you learn something new throughout your day without opening an app.

Features

  • Lock Screen Widget — Words and expressions with their translations, right under the clock
  • Words, Expressions, Phrasal Verbs & Idioms — With example sentences, pronunciation, and grammar tips
  • Your Languages — Choose your language and the language you want to learn
  • Favorites, History & Progress — Save what you like and see how much you have discovered
  • One-Time Purchase — Pay once. No subscriptions, no ads

Privacy First

LingoGlow works completely offline. There are no accounts, servers, or trackers. Your preferences, favorites, and progress never leave your iPhone.
