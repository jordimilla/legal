Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

LingoGlow ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection

LingoGlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no backend. Everything you do in the app stays exclusively on your device.

What Is Stored on Your Device

  • Your language choices and learning preferences.
  • Your favorite words and expressions.
  • Your learning history, progress, and streaks.
  • The upcoming schedule of words shown by the widget.

This information is saved in a storage area on your iPhone that is shared only between the LingoGlow app and its own widget (an App Group). It is never uploaded, synced to a cloud service, or shared with anyone. You can erase it at any time with "Reset local data" in Settings, or by deleting the app.

Offline Operation

All learning content (words, translations, and example sentences) is bundled with the app. LingoGlow works fully offline, including in Airplane Mode, and does not make network requests.

Lock Screen Widget

The widget displays words from the content bundled in the app, using the preferences stored on your device. It does not collect or send any information. The time at which iOS refreshes a widget is controlled by the system.

Pronunciation

The "Listen" feature uses the text-to-speech voices installed on your iPhone. Speech is generated on your device and no audio or text is sent anywhere. The app does not use the microphone.

Permissions

LingoGlow does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal data.

Purchases

LingoGlow is a paid app with a single up-front price, purchased through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, and the app contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple's privacy policy governs the purchase transaction.

Analytics and Advertising

LingoGlow does not include any third-party analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

LingoGlow does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app does not require account creation or personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
