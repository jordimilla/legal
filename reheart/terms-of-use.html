What Reheart is

Reheart is a self-help companion for people going through a breakup. It is not therapy, medical or psychological advice, and it does not replace a professional. It makes no promise about the outcome of any relationship or about your wellbeing. The test gives a perspective based on your own answers, not a prediction or an instruction.

Safety

If you feel unsafe, hopeless or in crisis, contact a professional or your local emergency number right away. Help lines inside the app come from official sources, but numbers, hours and costs can change and may not cover every region. You must never use the app to contact, follow, monitor or pressure someone who has asked for distance.
