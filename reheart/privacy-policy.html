Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad

Summary

Reheart keeps everything you enter on your device. The developer does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The app does not need an internet connection.

What is stored on your device

Only what you enter or generate: your answers to the daily tasks, your diary notes and moods, unsent letters, test answers and results, your streak and program progress, your SOS uses (day, hour and what you chose), and your settings. It is stored in one file in the app's private storage (shared only with Reheart's own widget through an App Group) with iOS file protection. Nothing you write is analysed: there is no artificial intelligence and no text analysis.

Widget

The widget shows your streak and the day number, with neutral text. It reads a small summary file on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks iOS for permission. Reminders are scheduled and shown on the iPhone itself; they say only that today's task is waiting. Nothing is sent to a server.

Face ID and passcode

The optional lock uses the iOS system authentication. Reheart never receives or stores your biometric data.

Sharing and export

The diary PDF is created on your device. It leaves the iPhone only if you choose to share it with the system share sheet. The test result card contains only the general result, never your answers.

Help lines

When you tap a help line, iOS opens the Phone app or your browser. Those services are independent of Reheart and have their own privacy practices.

Purchase

The purchase and its restoration are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Reheart only checks locally whether the full program is unlocked. The developer does not receive your payment information.

Children

Reheart is intended for adults and does not collect data from anyone, including minors.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all my data removes everything from the device. Deleting the app does too.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page and the App Store listing will be updated. Contact: jmillacatalan@gmail.com.

Se guarda solo lo que introduces: respuestas a las tareas, notas del diario y ánimo, cartas no enviadas, respuestas y resultados del test, racha y progreso, usos del SOS (día, hora y lo que elegiste) y ajustes, en un fichero privado de la app con protección de datos de iOS (compartido únicamente con el widget de Reheart).
