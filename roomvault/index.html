Quick answers

How do I make a list for my insurer? Open Reports, choose what to include and tap Create PDF. You can share it from the share sheet.

Can I back up my inventory? Settings > Data > Back up creates a file with all items, photos and documents. Keep it somewhere safe; you can restore it on another iPhone.

Why did I not get a warranty reminder? Check that notifications are allowed for Roomvault in iOS Settings and that the item has a warranty end date and a reminder set.
